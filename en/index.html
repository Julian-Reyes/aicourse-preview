Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The first lesson explains what programming is, and the full course has a fundamentals module before the projects. You learn to ask the AI for code and to understand what it wrote.

Will I have to pay for any tools?

You can take the course without paying anything: the "Which tool to use, and what it costs" lesson shows free options like Copilot CLI and Cursor. Claude Code, which the lessons use, starts at US$20/month (Claude Pro), or you buy credits and pay only for what you use.

Does it work on Windows, Mac, and Linux?

Yes. The install lessons have step-by-step instructions for each system.

Do I need a powerful computer?

No. The AI's heavy lifting happens on the company's servers, not on your computer. An ordinary laptop with internet is enough.

Are the lessons videos?

Rarely, and on purpose. The lessons are interactive: short text, code you edit right on the page, exercises, and quick questions. A short video shows up only when showing is easier than explaining. You learn by doing, at your own pace, and can find any part again in seconds.

How long does it take?

The mini-course takes about 80 minutes. The full course has about 21 hours of lessons, not counting the time you spend building the projects. You go at your own pace.

Is there a certificate?

Yes. When you finish a course, a certificate with your name and the study hours is ready to download from your dashboard.

How long do I have access?

On the monthly plan, while your subscription is active. On the annual plan, for 12 months. The free mini-course stays open forever.

What if I don't like it?

You have a 7-day guarantee: just ask for a refund and get 100% of your money back.

What language is the course in?

Brazilian Portuguese. Every lesson is also in English, and you can switch languages anytime at the top of the page.

How do I ask questions?

Email julian@julianreyes.dev. I answer personally.
